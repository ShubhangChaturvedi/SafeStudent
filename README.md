# SafeStudent – Student Safety & Well-Being Platform

SafeStudent is my full-stack web application designed to make every classroom safer and more supportive. The entire app runs on large classroom digital boards (and works on tablets/phones too), so students always have immediate, non-disruptive access to help — even during lessons.

I built this project from scratch because I strongly believe no student should ever feel alone or afraid to ask for help in school.

Live version: https://safestudent.created.app/

## Why I Created SafeStudent
During my time in school, I noticed many students hesitate to raise their hands when they feel sick, anxious, or need any kind of support. I wanted to create a solution that is always visible, judgment-free, and instantly available. SafeStudent turns every classroom screen into a “silent guardian”.

## Core Features I Designed & Implemented

- **AI-Powered Chatbot**  
  Built with Groq/Llama 3 – helps with homework, exam stress, bullying concerns, feeling unwell, or any question. Sensitive topics are flagged for counselor follow-up.

- **Silent “Request Assistance” Button**  
  Students can quietly notify teachers or counselors (“I feel sick”, “Need nurse”, “Personal issue”) without speaking or drawing attention.

- **One-Tap SOS Emergency Alert**  
  Instantly sends student’s location and emergency type to administrators and security.

- **Emergency Contacts Hub**  
  Direct one-tap access to police, ambulance, poison control, school nurse, counselors, and custom contacts.

- **Illustrated First-Aid Guides**  
  Step-by-step visual guides for common emergencies (asthma attacks, fractures, choking, seizures, allergies, etc.).

- **Live Weather & Real-Time School Alerts**  
  Severe weather warnings, lockdown drills, early dismissal, and daily announcements.

- **Classroom-Optimized UI**  
  Large text, high-contrast colors, and huge tap targets specifically designed for wall-mounted digital boards.

## Technology & Development
- Built instantly with **create.xyz** (visual editor + custom HTML/CSS/JS)
- Integrated GPT-4 family model for fast AI responses
- Fully responsive PWA
- Single-file deployment for instant school-wide rollout

## Impact Goal
I want SafeStudent to be used in real schools to reduce response time in emergencies and make mental-health support feel normal and accessible. The platform requires zero installation and can be customized for any school in minutes.
